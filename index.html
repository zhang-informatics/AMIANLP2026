Introduction

Biomedical NLP in a changing health AI landscape.

The application of Natural Language Processing (NLP) in everyday life has rapidly progressed over the past decades. This progress has been enabled by the availability of tools, large language models, and resources that could be shared, reused, and fine-tuned to support users' projects and collaborations. Today, recently evolved large generative language models are being integrated into general search and workplace productivity tools.

Applying NLP to the textual content of patient electronic health records, clinical text, is constrained by strict patient privacy and confidentiality laws and regulations. These domain-specific peculiarities for access and sharing of resources, such as annotated text corpora, and tools, such as trained machine learning algorithms, require creative solutions.

The workshop aims to provide a platform for the next generation of biomedical NLP scientists to get focused feedback on in-progress graduate work, provide a forum for state-of-the-art NLP and LLM work, and discuss promises and challenges related to emerging generative AI technologies, especially in health systems and adjacent industries.

Graduate Student Consortium

Graduate students are invited to submit applications for a podium presentation of their graduate research work in the biomedical and clinical NLP fields. The submission is suggested to include:

  • Aims and objectives
  • Justification for the research topic
  • Research questions
  • Research methodology
  • Research results to date
  • References

State-of-the-Art Biomedical and Clinical NLP

Researchers are encouraged to submit recent research studies, tools, resources, events, and community shared tasks. The following sections are suggested:

  • Methods, tools, resources, events, or shared tasks description
  • Justification of the inclusion
  • Summary or outcome
  • References
